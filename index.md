# Ribbon Journal

A paper notebook for Apple Pencil. Open it and you're on today's page, ready
to write. It keeps itself: there's no save button.

## Support

Questions, problems or ideas: email
[support@a14ylabs.com](mailto:support@a14ylabs.com).

**How do I turn the page?** Swipe in from the edge of the page, or tap its
folded corner. A new page appears once you've written on the last one.

**Where are the other things?** Tap the red ribbon at the top of the page:
your notebooks, the calendar, search, PDF export, paper styles and the lock
are all there.

**Can I write with my finger?** Yes. Apple Pencil writes, and a finger writes
too until you first use a Pencil; after that a finger turns pages.

**How do my pages get to my other devices?** Through your iCloud account,
with nothing to set up. Sign in to iCloud on each device. Nothing you write
is ever lost to syncing.

**How do I search my handwriting?** Tap the ribbon, then Search. Your
handwriting is read on your device, a few seconds after you write.

**How do I lock my journal?** Tap the ribbon and turn on the lock. Face ID or
your passcode opens it again.

**How do I delete everything?** Delete the app, then delete Ribbon Journal's
data from iCloud in Settings, under your Apple Account's iCloud storage. See
the [privacy policy](privacy/) for what's kept and why.

## Privacy

Ribbon Journal collects no data. Read the [privacy policy](privacy/).
